Heroes

Camus ends the book with Sisyphus, but the chapter before it, “The Absurd Man”, has four more absurd heroes. Each one lives without a final meaning in a different way. Push boulders on the timer to unlock them, then pick who carries the rock.

Choosing Class demo in the timer’s Settings unlocks all five, for presenting.

Camus quotations from The Myth of Sisyphus (1942), translated by Justin O’Brien (1955).
