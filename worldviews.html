Seven worldviews, one rock

The same endless task, seen by seven traditions. Camus answers with revolt. The others accept it, hope beyond it, step away from it, do it as a duty, or simply pay attention to it. After each climb, the timer tells you who stands with your answer and who would argue back.

Who would push your rock?

Seven situations from student life, seven ways to answer each. At the end you find out which of the seven you sound like, who would argue back, and whether you push the way you answer.
