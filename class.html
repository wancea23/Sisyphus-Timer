How it works

  1. I

    The presenter starts a class

    A QR code and a 4-letter code appear, big enough for the back row.

  2. II

    Everyone answers on their phone

    One question, Camus’ three answers, and an optional sentence. No names are collected.

  3. III

    The room sees itself

    The projector shows the split between escape, hope and revolt, with the written answers. The presenter can hide any answer and start a new round.

  4. IV

    Or take the worldview test together

    The presenter switches the room to the test. Everyone answers seven situations on their phone, and the projector shows which of the seven worldviews the class sounds like.
